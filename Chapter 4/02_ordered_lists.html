Steps to Bake a Cake

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Grease and flour a cake pan.
  3. In a bowl, mix the dry ingredients together.
  4. In another bowl, beat the eggs and sugar until fluffy.
  5. Add the wet ingredients to the dry ingredients and mix well.
  6. Pour the batter into the prepared cake pan.
  7. Bake for 30-35 minutes or until a toothpick comes out clean.
  8. Let the cake cool before serving.
