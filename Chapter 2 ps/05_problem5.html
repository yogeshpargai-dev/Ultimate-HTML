Virat Kohli

Virat Kohli is an Indian international cricketer who currently serves as the captain of the India national team. He is a right-handed batsman and a slow left-arm orthodox spin bowler.

Early Life

Virat Kohli was born on November 5, 1988, in Delhi, India. He developed an interest in cricket at a young age and joined the West Delhi Cricket Academy to pursue his passion for the sport.

Domestic Career

Kohli made his debut in domestic cricket for Delhi in 2006. He quickly rose through the ranks and became one of the most promising young talents in Indian cricket.

International Career

Kohli made his international debut for India in 2008. He has since become one of the most successful batsmen in the world, known for his aggressive style of play and consistency in scoring runs across all formats of the game.

Achievements

  • ICC ODI Player of the Year: 2012, 2017
  • ICC Test Player of the Year: 2018
  • Fastest century in ODIs by an Indian: 52 balls
  • Most double centuries in a calendar year in Tests: 3 (2016)

Personal Life

Kohli married Bollywood actress Anushka Sharma in December 2017. The couple welcomed their first child, a daughter named Vamika, in January 2021.
